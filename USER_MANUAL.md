## Getting Started

1. **System Requirements**
   - Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
   - Internet connection
   - Screen resolution of 1024x768 or higher

2. **Accessing the Application**
   - Open your web browser and navigate to [application-url]
   - Register for a new account or log in if you already have one

## Creating an Account

1. Click on "Sign Up" on the login page
2. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name
   - Email Address
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm Password
3. Click "Create Account"
4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your email

## Generating a Workout Plan

1. Navigate to "Workout Plan" in the main menu
2. Select your fitness level:
   - Beginner
   - Intermediate
   - Advanced
3. Choose your training days per week (3-6 days)
4. Select your primary fitness goals:
   - Muscle Building
   - Strength
   - Endurance
   - Fat Loss
5. Click "Generate Plan"

## Nutrition Profile Setup

1. Go to "Profile" in the main menu
2. Click on "Set Up Nutrition Profile"
3. Enter your details:
   - Age (18-80)
   - Height (120-230 cm)
   - Weight (40-250 kg)
   - Gender
   - Activity Level
   - Fitness Goal (Lose/Maintain/Gain weight)
4. Click "Save Profile"

## Viewing Your Workout Plan

1. Your workout plan is displayed on the Profile page
2. Each session shows:
   - Session number
   - Focus areas
   - List of exercises with sets and reps
   - Alternative exercises (if available)
3. Use the navigation to switch between different workout days

## Tracking Progress

1. **Logging Workouts**
   - Mark exercises as completed
   - Track weights and reps
   - Add notes for each session

2. **Nutrition Tracking**
   - View daily calorie and macro targets
   - Log meals and track nutrition
   - Monitor progress towards goals

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

1. **Can't log in**
   - Ensure caps lock is off
   - Check your internet connection
   - Click "Forgot Password" if needed

2. **Workout plan not loading**
   - Refresh the page
   - Check your internet connection
   - Ensure you're logged in

3. **Exercise demonstration not playing**
   - Check your internet connection
   - Ensure your browser supports HTML5 video
   - Try a different browser
